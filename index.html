Why Auto-Reply Messages Matter

An automatic WhatsApp reply sets expectations immediately, even when nobody is available to answer. Customers are far more likely to wait for a real response if they get instant confirmation that their message was received and roughly when to expect a follow-up.

Using These With WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp Business lets you set an Away Message and a Greeting Message directly in the app under Business Settings, or you can wire these templates into a chatbot flow if you are using the WhatsApp Business API.
